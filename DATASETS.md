# Datasets

Raw benchmark datasets are **not stored in this public repository**.

## CICIDS2017

Official source: https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2017.html

Place the extracted CICIDS2017 CSV files under:

```text
data/cicids2017/raw/
```

The public repository does not redistribute the dataset. Please use the official source and cite the dataset/research paper according to the provider's requirements.

## UNSW-NB15

Official source: https://research.unsw.edu.au/projects/unsw-nb15-dataset

Expected files:

```text
data/unsw_nb15/UNSW_NB15_training-set.csv
data/unsw_nb15/UNSW_NB15_testing-set.csv
```

The dissertation used a proportional stratified 60,000-record sample of the official training data and retained the full official 82,332-record test set.

UNSW states that the dataset is available for academic/public research use and provides citation requirements on the official dataset page. Commercial use should follow the terms stated by the dataset authors.
